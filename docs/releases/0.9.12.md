# 通讯互联 CommLink 0.9.12

## 中文

- 移除云短信网关、Webhook 和旧的独立心跳检测，清理对应的设置入口和后台任务。
- 从设备在同步空闲时减少轮询，短信或通讯录变化会唤醒同步。
- 调整主从设备的后台健康检查，复用主设备守护已完成的 HTTPS 探测，并在从设备检查时通知同步服务。

可覆盖安装，保留短信、配对和设置。iPhone 通知、主从同步心跳和 SIP 通话继续可用。使用旧 Webhook 接口的外部程序需调整集成。

## English

- Removes the cloud SMS gateway, webhooks, and the legacy standalone heartbeat, including their settings and background jobs.
- Reduces idle polling on secondary devices. SMS and contact changes wake the sync service.
- Reuses HTTPS health probes on the primary device and wakes the sync service during checks on secondary devices.

Install over the existing APK to keep messages, pairings, and settings. iPhone notifications, peer sync heartbeats, and SIP calling remain available. External integrations that use the legacy webhook endpoints need to be updated.
