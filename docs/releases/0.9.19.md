# 通讯互联 CommLink 0.9.19

## 中文

- 从 iPhone 主屏幕打开「互联电话」，直接显示拨号键盘。线路加载完成后再启用操作，去掉可能一闪而过的「互联电话」等待页。
- 点按未接来电通知，直接进入通话记录。
- 移除拨号键盘下方的 Groundwire 常驻说明。需要处理的线路和权限提示仍会显示。

## English

- Opening Phone from the iPhone Home Screen goes straight to the keypad. Controls become available once the lines have loaded, with no separate startup screen.
- Tapping a missed-call notification opens call history directly.
- Removes the persistent Groundwire note below the keypad. Line and permission warnings still appear when needed.
