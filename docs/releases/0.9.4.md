# CommLink 通讯互联 0.9.4

## 中文

版本号 0.9.4，versionCode 56。简化 iPhone 短信页面的 SIM 显示。

- 设置只显示在位 SIM，没插卡的卡槽不显示条目，移除“历史线路”区域。
- 短信发送菜单同样隐藏已不在位的线路。已选线路拔卡后显示“原线路不可用”，需要用户重新选择，不自动换卡发送。
- 待确认、停用及离线提示继续保留。旧线路标识和历史短信关联保存在后台。

同签名覆盖升级保留配对、名称、短信和确认状态。更新后重新打开 iPhone 短信页面即可。浏览器回归覆盖同卡槽旧记录隐藏、全部拔卡后设置留空、原选择不可用及发送禁用，不发送真实短信。

## English

Version 0.9.4, versionCode 56, simplifies SIM presentation in iPhone Messages.

- Settings show present SIMs only. Empty slots have no entries, and the historical-lines section is removed.
- SMS line selectors also hide absent lines. A removed selected SIM stays unavailable until the user explicitly selects another line.
- Confirmation, disabled and offline labels remain. Stable historical line identities and message associations stay in the backend.

An in-place update retains signing identity, pairing, names, SMS and confirmation choices. Reopen the iPhone web app after updating. Browser regression checks cover hidden old subscriptions on the same slot, an empty SIM area after removal, preservation of an unavailable selection and disabled sending. No real SMS is sent.
