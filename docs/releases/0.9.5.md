# CommLink 通讯互联 0.9.5

## 中文

版本号 0.9.5，versionCode 57。补齐电话页面的未插卡显示处理，与短信页面保持一致。

- 电话设置、拨出线路和联系人首选线路只显示在位 SIM。短信和电话使用相同的在位判断。
- 拔卡后保留原选择的不可用提示，不自动改用另一条线路；已不在位或停用的线路不能生成 Groundwire 拨号链接，即使残留的路由仍可见。
- 电话设置区分待确认、已停用、手机离线和暂不可用状态。所有卡都拔出时，设备下不显示 SIM 条目。
- 历史通话继续使用记录中的手机与卡槽，不根据当前线路列表重新分配。

同签名覆盖升级保留配对、线路名称、确认状态、短信和通话数据。更新后重新打开 iPhone 的电话和短信页面即可。前端验证覆盖同一卡槽旧线路隐藏、待确认线路、全部拔卡、旧选择禁拨、历史 SIM 来源及短信页面回归。不拨打真实电话或发送真实短信。

## English

Version 0.9.5, versionCode 57, completes absent-SIM handling in Phone and shares the presence rule with Messages.

- Phone settings, outgoing-line choices and contact preferences show present SIMs only.
- Removing a selected SIM leaves an unavailable preference rather than selecting a different line. Absent or disabled lines cannot generate Groundwire links, even if a stale route remains visible.
- Phone settings distinguish confirmation, disabled, device-offline and unavailable states. Devices with no present SIMs have no SIM rows.
- Historical calls retain their recorded source device and slot independently of current line choices.

An in-place update retains signing identity, pairing, names, confirmation choices, SMS and call data. Reopen both iPhone web apps after updating. Frontend validation covers hidden old subscriptions on the same slot, unconfirmed lines, removal of every card, blocked stale choices, historical provenance and Messages regression. No actual calls or SMS are placed.
